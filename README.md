# klingon
Copyright (C) 1976.12.27 TOP WAYE topwaye@hotmail.com

basic human brain language logic shown by a recursive algorithm running on a basic CPU architecture

**give some words to klingon:**

>play what  
at home  
bad  
playing cards  

**klingon gives you sentences:**

>what do you play at home  
would you have been playing cards at home  
what do you think you play at home  
you aren't at home  
not bad  
don't be bad  
my love shall in my verse ever live young  
are you singing or are you dancing  
who is he  
who is bad  
who do you want to be  
who do you want to love  
who do you want to play with  
with who do you want to play  
do you want not to be loved  

klingon shows how our brains actually think to form sentences

all the thinking in our brains is just mathematical permutations and combinations

gcc main.c -o main
